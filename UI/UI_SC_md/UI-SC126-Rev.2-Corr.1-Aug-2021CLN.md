# SC 126

(May 1998)
(Rev.1 June 2000)
(Rev.2 Nov 2005)
(Corr.1 Aug 2021)

## Fire Protection Materials for Cargo Ships (SOLAS regulations II-2/4.4.4, 5.3, 6.2.1 and 6.3.1)

For the application of SOLAS regulations II-2/4.4.4, 5.3, 6.2.1 and 6.3.1, reference is to be made to the attached Figure 1 and associated Tables 1 and 2.

![Figure 1: Cross-sectional diagram of constructional elements in accommodation spaces, service spaces and control stations (left) and corridors/stairway enclosures (right), with numbered callouts 1-18 indicating components such as moulding, panel, painted surfaces, decoration, skirting board, insulation, draught stops, grounds and supports, lining, primary deck covering, floor finishing, window box and ceiling panel, including concealed or inaccessible spaces](assets/UI-SC126-Rev.2-Corr.1-Aug-2021CLN/part01-fig-000-merged.png)

Figure 1: Constructural elements in accommodation spaces

Note:

1. This Unified Interpretation is to be applied by all Members and Associates on ships contracted for construction on or after 1 January 2006.
2. The "contracted for construction" date means the date on which the contract to build the vessel is signed between the prospective owner and the shipbuilder. For further details regarding the date of "contract for construction", refer to IACS Procedural Requirement (PR) No. 29."
