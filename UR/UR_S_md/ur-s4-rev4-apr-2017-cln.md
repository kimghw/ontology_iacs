# S4 Criteria for the Use of High Tensile Steel with Minimum Yield Stress of 315 N/mm<sup>2</sup>, 355 N/mm<sup>2</sup> and 390 N/mm<sup>2</sup>

S4
(1973)
(Rev.1 1974)
(Rev.2 Apr 2007)
(Rev.3 May 2010)
(Rev.4 Apr 2017)

This UR does not apply to CSR Bulk Carriers and Oil Tankers.

The material factor *k* is defined as follows:

- *k* = 0.78 for steel with *R*<sub>eH</sub> = 315 N/mm<sup>2</sup>
- *k* = 0.72 for steel with *R*<sub>eH</sub> = 355 N/mm<sup>2</sup>
- *k* = 0.66 for steel with *R*<sub>eH</sub> = 390 N/mm<sup>2</sup> provided that a fatigue assessment of the structure is performed to verify compliance with the requirements of the Society,
- *k* = 0.68 for steel with *R*<sub>eH</sub> = 390 N/mm<sup>2</sup> in other cases.

Where:

*R*<sub>eH</sub> : Minimum yield stress, in N/mm<sup>2</sup>
