## Application

This Procedure contains procedures and requirements pertaining to assigning class for a newbuilding project by Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) adopted between a Class Society, a Shipyard and, if applicable, a Ship Owner covering the acceptance by the Society of a design based on the prior approval by an Initial Society for a new building.

The obligations of this Procedure apply to Classification Societies which are subject to verification of compliance with the Quality System Certification Scheme (QSCS) in accordance with Section 5 of Annex 1 to the QSCS.

## Definitions

'Initial Society' is the Society that has previously approved the design using their own Rules.

## Section A - Assigning Class for a New Building project Based on the Classification Rules and a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) Between a Class Society, a Shipyard, and if applicable, a Ship Owner

A.1 Whenever a Society is requested by an Owner/Shipyard to class a vessel(s) for new construction project when the design is already approved by an Initial Society, which can provide satisfactory evidence of continued compliance with QSCS, the following procedures are to be applied.

1. the Society may accept to class a new building project vessel when the design is already approved by an Initial Society for new building and all comments are confirmed as dealt with by the Initial Society approving the design, subject to the requirements in .3, .4 and .5 being fulfilled;

2. the Society may accept to class a new building project vessel in accordance with the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) adopted by the Society and the shipyard, subject to the requirements in .3, .4 and .5 being fulfilled. This MoU shall clearly define requirements for submission of plans as required by the Rules of the Society, harmonizing and resolution of plans already approved by an Initial Society for new building project and comments to be dealt with. A format for the minimum content of an MoU is provided in Annex.

3. the Society is to perform review and approval of plans based on its own class Rules. As a minimum scope, the approval of the plans listed in Section B is required by the Society to verify compliance with its applicable classification Rules. The Society is to record the written documentary evidence of the above-mentioned plans which were approved as complying with the Society's own Rules or with other requirements confirmed acceptable in accordance with its own Rules;

4. the Society is to perform the survey during fabrication, construction and testing of the vessel based on its own classification Rules;

5. Certification of materials and components of manufacturers shall be carried out by the Society in accordance with its own classification Rules;

6. the Society is to issue its certificate of classification in accordance with its own classification Rules for the vessel upon satisfactory completion of new construction survey process; and

7. records in relation to the above-mentioned paragraphs are maintained, demonstrating achievement of the requirements in the items covered by the services performed, as well as the effective operation of the quality system.

## Section B - Plans to be submitted by the Shipyard to the Society

### B.1 Main, Steel/Structural, Machinery and Electrical Plans

#### B.1.1 Main plans

- General Arrangement
- Capacity Plan
- Hydrostatic Curves
- Loading Manual, where required.
- Damage Stability calculation, where required.

#### B.1.2 Steel plans

- Midship Section
- Scantling Plan
- Decks
- Shell Expansion
- Transverse Bulkheads
- Rudder and Rudder Stock
- Hatch Covers
- For CSR vessels, plans showing, for each structural element, both as-built and renewal thicknesses and any thickness for 'voluntary addition'.
- Plan of tank testing

#### B.1.3 Machinery and Electrical plans

- Machinery Arrangement
- Intermediate, Thrust- and Screw Shafts
- Propeller
- Couplings and shaft alignment calculation
- Main Engines, Propulsion Gears and Clutch Systems (or Manufacturer make, model and rating information)
- For Steam Turbine Vessels, Main Boilers, Superheaters and Economisers (or Manufacturer make, model and rating information) and Steam Piping
- Bilge and Ballast Piping Diagram
- Wiring Diagram
- Steering Gear Systems Piping and Arrangements and Steering Gear Manufacturer make and model information
- Diagram of the air, sounding and overflow systems
- Diagram of cooling systems (sea water and fresh water)
- Diagram of fuel oil system
- Diagram of the lubricating oil system
- Diagram of the hydraulic systems intended for essential services or located in machinery spaces
- Diagrams of sea water and / or freshwater piping systems
- Electrical power balance (main and emergency supply)
- General specification for the automation of the ship
- Detailed specification of the essential service systems
- List of components used in the automation circuits, and references (Manufacturer, type, etc.)
- General diagrams showing the monitoring and/or control positions for the various installations, with an indication of the means of access and the means of communication between the positions as well as with the engineers
- Diagrams of the supply circuits of automation systems, identifying the power source
- List of monitored parameters for alarm/monitoring and safety systems
- Diagram of compressed air system

### B.2 Torsional vibration calculations

B.2.1 Torsional vibration calculations.

### B.3 Additional requirements for vessels with ice class notation

B.3.1 Plans for flexible couplings and/or torque limiting shafting devices in the propulsion line shafting (or manufacturer make, model and rating information).

### B.4 Additional plans required for oil tankers

B.4.1 Pumping arrangement at the forward and after ends and drainage of cofferdams and pump rooms.

### B.5 Additional plans required for unattended machinery space notation

B.5.1

- Instrument and Alarm List
- Fire Alarm System
- List of Automatic Safety Functions (e.g. slowdowns, shutdowns, etc.)
- Function Testing Plan.

### B.6 Additional Documents required for approval of Alternative Design and Arrangements

B.6.1 Document(s) of Approval of Alternative Design and Arrangements, if any.

### B.7 Expansion of the list of plans

This list shall be expanded depending on the type of ship under the responsibility of the Society.

## ANNEX

### MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

### Classification process for New Ship Building

Hull Number XX

This Memorandum of Understanding (the "**MoU**") was made on signature date by and between Class Society XXX (the "**Society**"), Shipyard XXX and, [if applicable] Shipowner XXXX, for the Classification process of the New Building XX with acknowledgement of Design prior approval by an Initial Class Society (the "**Initial Society**").

### 1. Submission and approval of plans and documents:

- The Shipyard shall provide the Society with a copy of all plans, supporting documents and calculations previously approved by the Initial Society (including comments) for reference.
- The Shipyard shall submit all plans, supporting documents and calculations required for Classification to the Society for review and approval. Changes or modifications of any part of the design by the Shipyard, shall be clearly marked on the drawings. As a minimum scope, the approval of the plans listed in Section B of PR42 is required by the Society to verify compliance with its own Classification Rules applicable.
- Further to the review performed by the Society in accordance with PR42, if any deviations from the Rules are identified or discrepancies are found between the design and applicable statutory regulations for which the Society is authorized to confirm compliance, the Society shall promptly inform the Shipyard and both parties shall co-operate to solve the matter, in order for the Shipyard to take appropriate corrective actions as relevant.

### 2. Verification of construction and testing:

- The Society shall conduct the Classification survey during construction in accordance with its own Classification Rules.

### 3. Issuance of Certificates:

- Certification of materials and components (approval of plans and survey) shall be carried out by the Society in accordance with its own Classification Rules.
- Subject to the satisfactory completion of Society's Classification process, the Classification certificate, shall be issued in accordance with the Society's own classification Rules.

### 4. Conditions:

- This MoU shall be effective from the date the MoU has been signed by the Shipyard and the Society. It will only bind the Society when the Classification Service Agreement is signed, and shall continue until all obligations as stated in this MoU have been fulfilled.
- This MoU is an addendum to the Classification Service Agreement and remains subject to the terms and conditions of the Classification Service Agreement. All definitions included in this MoU shall have the same meaning as described in the Classification Service Agreement.
- In case of any termination of the Classification Service Agreement, this MoU shall immediately be terminated.
- For all matters related to the Vessel's classification, the Society's decision shall be final as provided in its Rules, and in all matters related to statutory certification, the relevant flag Administration shall be the final decision authority.
- Except as expressly stated otherwise, nothing in this MoU shall be deemed to amend or supersede the Classification Service Agreement conditions.
