## Table 3 – Symbols and subscripts for terms and variables (refer to chapter 5, chapter 6, appendix 4 and appendix 6 of this Code)

Table 3 gives:

| Symbol | Term | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *T<sub>a</sub>* | Intake air temperature determined at the engine intake | K |

### Interpretation

For application of the term "*T<sub>a</sub>*" it shall be interpreted that the intake air temperature determined at the engine intake is that determined at the engine / turbocharger intake suction filter.
